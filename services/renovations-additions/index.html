How we protect your existing home

Every renovation starts with a structural assessment before we touch a single wall. We identify load-bearing elements, existing electrical and plumbing routing, and anything code-related that a fast estimate would miss. That assessment becomes part of your fixed-bid quote, so what you sign is what you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Can we still live in the house during the renovation?

For most kitchen and bathroom projects, yes — we phase work to keep at least one functional bathroom and a temporary kitchen setup available. Whole-home renovations and second-story additions usually require temporary relocation; we'll be upfront about that during your quote.

Do you handle the permits for additions?

Yes — additions and structural changes require permits in every municipality we work in, and we pull and manage all of them as part of the project.

How do you price renovations if you don't know what's behind the walls?

Our structural walkthrough plus a contingency line item (typically 10–15% for older homes) covers this. If we find something unexpected, it's handled through a documented change order, never a surprise invoice.
