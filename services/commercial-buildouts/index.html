How phased construction works

For occupied spaces, we break the buildout into zones and schedule work zone-by-zone around your posted hours. For a typical retail buildout, that means framing and mechanical work after close, with finish work (flooring, fixtures, paint) completed before you reopen each section. You'll get a zone-by-zone schedule as part of your proposal, not a vague "a few weeks" estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build out a space I haven't signed a lease on yet?

Yes — we regularly provide buildout cost estimates during lease negotiations, so you know your total occupancy cost before you sign.

Do you handle permits for commercial spaces?

Yes, including any required fire marshal or health department inspections for food service and other regulated occupancy types.

What's a typical timeline for a retail buildout?

A standard 1,500–3,000 sq ft retail or office buildout typically runs 6–10 weeks from permit approval, depending on the scope of mechanical and electrical work required.
