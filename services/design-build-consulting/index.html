Services / Design-Build Consulting

Get a second opinion before you break ground

A lot of budget overruns get baked into a project before construction even starts. Our consulting service reviews your plans, your numbers, and your timeline against what actually gets built in the field.

Book a Consultation

What a consultation covers

  • A buildability review of your existing architectural drawings
  • A realistic cost range based on current material and labor pricing — not a three-year-old rule of thumb
  • Permitting risk flags specific to your municipality and lot
  • A phasing recommendation if your budget doesn't cover the full scope at once
  • A written summary you can take to another contractor if you decide not to build with us — no obligation either way

Who this is for

Homeowners who already have architectural drawings and want a contractor's-eye review before bidding it out. Business owners evaluating a lease who need real buildout numbers before signing. Anyone who's gotten wildly different quotes from different contractors and wants to understand why.

Frequently asked questions

Does this cost anything?

The initial consultation is free. If your project needs a deeper feasibility study — structural engineering review, detailed takeoffs — we'll quote that separately and you decide whether to proceed.

Am I obligated to hire Ironpeak afterward?

No. You'll get a written summary of our findings regardless of who ends up building the project.

I don't have architectural drawings yet — can you still help?

Yes. We can point you toward design partners we trust, or do a rough feasibility pass on hand sketches or reference photos before you invest in full drawings.
