  • Permitting handled start to finish — we pull every permit ourselves

Typical timeline

Most custom homes in the 2,500–4,500 sq ft range take 9–14 months from permit approval to final inspection, depending on lot conditions and finish-level selections. We'll give you a realistic, project-specific schedule in your quote — not a marketing-brochure estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle the architectural design too?

We work with a small group of architects and designers we trust, and can bring your own architect in if you already have one. Either way, we're involved early enough to flag cost and buildability issues before they're locked into the drawings.

Can I make changes once construction starts?

Yes — through a formal change order that documents the cost and schedule impact before any work proceeds, so there's never a surprise on your final invoice.

Do you build in areas outside Denver proper?

We regularly build throughout the greater Denver metro, including Golden, Wheat Ridge, Lakewood, and Arvada. Reach out with your lot's location and we'll confirm.
